⚠ Important Notice: Digital Attendance Guidelines
Dear Staff and Employees,

We wish to inform everyone that the new Digital Attendance System has been introduced primarily to improve efficiency, reduce the use of paper attendance, and support better record keeping within the Commission.

This system is designed to be friendly and supportive, not strict or punitive. Its purpose is to modernize attendance processes, make record management easier, and improve accountability in a convenient way for everyone.

Kindly note that official work starts at 8:00 AM, and staff are expected to clock in and clock out within the approved time periods:
• Clock-in: 8:00 AM – 9:30 AM
• Clock-out: From 4:00 PM

We kindly encourage all staff to cooperate and make use of the system as part of our effort to improve operations within the Commission. Your understanding, patience, and feedback during this transition are highly appreciated.

Thank you for your cooperation.
